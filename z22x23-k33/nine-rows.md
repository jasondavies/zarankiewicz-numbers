# Every 217-one rectangle has at least four nine-rows and an eleven-or-longer row

All rows have degree at least nine. Let k be the number of eight-columns, which is one or two. Deleting any one of them leaves a 209-one square. In such a square there is at most one eight-row, because deleting one leaves a 201-one rectangle whose rows all have degree at least nine. Thus each eight-column of the full rectangle hits at most one nine-row. A nine-row cannot meet both eight-columns, since deleting both would leave a row of degree seven in a 22 × 21 rectangle of weight 201 whose rows have degree at least eight. [201-one parent minimum](../z21x22-k33/parent-minimum.md) · [217 deletion bounds](README.md)

Each nine-row meets at most seven columns of degree at least ten. If it avoids the eight-columns it has nine high entries, and their total column excess above nine is at most seven. Otherwise delete the eight-column it meets, then the residual eight-row. The resulting 201-one matrix on 21 × 22 has at least three eight-columns. At most k−1 already had degree eight before the row deletion. The deleted row therefore contained at least 4−k nine-columns, as well as its original eight-column. In this case at most 4+k of its entries could be on columns of degree at least ten. [High-column weight](min9.md) · [Three eight-columns](../z21x22-k33/three-low-columns.md)

Suppose there were no row of degree at least eleven. Then there would be exactly three nine-rows and nineteen ten-rows. A column of degree at least eleven cannot contain six ten-rows, so it would require at least six nine-rows. Hence the columns have degrees eight, nine and ten, with exactly 10+k tens by the total weight. On a ten-column there are at least seven ten-rows. The ten-row mark for a column containing at least five high rows bounds the incident sum of row excesses above nine by eight. Thus every ten-column contains at least two nine-rows. Counting these entries gives at least 2(10+k) but at most 3·7, a contradiction. [Marked-column bounds](min9.md)

An eleven-or-longer row forces at least four nine-rows by the total of 217. Deleting each nine-row produces an extremal 208-one 21 × 23 matrix if a 217-one matrix exists. Every such parent has minimum row degree nine and accepts the deleted nine-row. Different deletions can give isomorphic parents.
